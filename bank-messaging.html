Bank Messaging

Rebuilding trust in fraud and scam alerts across platforms.

Lead researcher · 10 weeks · Qualitative + quantitative research · Cross-platform alert messaging

Context

Fraud and scams cost people billions annually. With 30+ fraud communication types, it was time to standardize our approach in order to increase message credibility and customer engagement.

Business Goal

Increase digital customer engagement to lower call volume and prevent fraud and scam losses.

Research Goal

Understand what drives customer trust in our messages.

Impact Preview

My research led to:

  • Progress towards an external partnership
  • Comprehensive messaging strategy
  • Brand design decisions

Constraints

  • Limited time and budget for extensive project scope
  • Inability to simulate accurate situations in testing
Alignment workspace: Defining four message dimensions and a menu of study options

Approach

After stakeholder interviews, I pitched a new research approach to identify what actually drives trust. We aligned on:

Click test: Testing two design versions with a time limit. 600 digitally active users across varying scam awareness levels could help us understand how security perception significantly affects reactions during urgent communications.

Lean think-aloud study: Behavioral research with an equal split of 70 mobile and web users with mixed scam experience to uncover patterns across platforms.

Key Findings

Click test results revealed critical insights about behavior under pressure

Button design didn't affect accuracy (50% vs. 51.8%) or speed across versions, which led to a confident design decision.

However, the low level of accuracy and 21-27% of user clicks on incorrect CTAs formed a behavioral hypothesis: Users struggle to identify their expected action in urgent circumstances.

Trust is the foundation of message performance

I identified key trust signals that influence whether users engage with messages, and outlined more use cases where customers are less likely to trust or respond.

Language educating customers about scams was an unexpectedly powerful trust signal. In the words of a participant:

A scammer wouldn't tell you that.

Outcomes

Comprehensive fraud messaging strategy

I delivered a full strategy document for scam and fraud messaging that simplifies designs for time-sensitive messaging, and enhances communication around consequences and alternative paths. The team is currently implementing recommendations which are projected to reduce call volume and prevent customer losses.

Sender verification partnership launched

Research insights on channel vulnerabilities directly informed a new sender verification partnership.

Design system validation

My findings confirmed that brand visual design doesn't compromise accuracy in urgent scenarios, giving the team an easy design win.

Reflection

If I were to do this project again, I would set out to define the dimensions of a successful communication earlier.
