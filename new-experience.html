New Vendor Experience

Research to bring a new partner experience to our platforms.

Lead researcher · 6 weeks · Qualitative research

Context

The company was considering taking on a new vendor to provide a white-labeled experience with a variety of features.

Research would help the two companies make decisions on their partnership. Our stakeholders needed to understand the usability and branding strengths of the new experience. Results would provide insights on where my company should push for design customization.

Research Goal: Uncover how our customers interact with a proposed new experience in order to inform a vendor partnership.

Constraints

  • The company had to collaborate with the vendor, so templates were closed off to most design changes.
  • My company’s platforms had signficant technical implementation limitations.

Approach

  • The complexity of the requirements led me to recommend and conduct in-depth interviews.
  • I partnered with a designer to build a medium fidelity prototype to capture the possible integration between our platforms and the new feature.

Key Findings

  • Our customers identified the experience as “belonging” to our company, thanks to the design integration and branding.
  • Thus, participants expected to locate a certain vendor feature where they typically use a similar bank feature (Feature A).
  • Layout and design made one of the vendor's features (Feature B) difficult to use.

Outcomes

The usability success of my testing made the company partnership possible, and feature launch is actually happening this week.

Our findings led to productive discussions with the vendor. They were able to accommodate design changes around the design limitations we documented in Feature B.

Due to technical constraints, my company was not able to implement an entry point for Feature A that would meet the participant expectations found in our study.

Reflection

This study reminded me of an important lesson: sometimes you have to compromise on an idealized user experience to get an MVP product to release.

This project made its way up the chain of command to highlight the potential dangers of flawed integration for Feature A. I worked with the team and other researchers to ensure we were providing an accurate risk assessment that weighed our recommendation against the real possibility that consumers could acclimate to a new entry point.
